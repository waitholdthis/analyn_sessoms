4x ICON Award Realtor® · eXp Realty

Analyn Sessoms is a 4x ICON Award–winning Residential Realtor with eXp Realty, serving Sanford, Lee County, and the surrounding Central North Carolina communities. Her motto says it all: "Sharing Cultures, Connecting Homes."

A passionate advocate for military families, Analyn specializes in VA home loans and understands firsthand what it means to serve — and to be served well. Whether you're a veteran buying your first home or a family upsizing in Pittsboro, she brings the same tenacity and heart to every transaction.

Recognized four consecutive times (2022, 2023, 2024 & 2025) at eXp Realty's highest production level, Analyn's track record is built on deep community knowledge, relentless negotiation, and a genuine commitment to every client she represents.
